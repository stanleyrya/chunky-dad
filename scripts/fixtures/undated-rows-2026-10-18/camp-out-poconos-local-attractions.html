Planning a visit to Camp Out Poconos?

Explore some of our favorite local spots, from scenic hikes and charming towns to breweries, vintage shopping, and LGBTQ+-friendly destinations. Whether you’re here for the weekend or the season, there’s plenty to discover just beyond camp.

Local Attractions

  • Downtown Stroudsburg – Have fun shopping, eating, drinking, and exploring your way around the historic town of Stroudsburg.
  • Bushkill Falls – “The Niagara of Pennsylvania” is a series of 8 waterfalls accessible by hiking through the wooded Pocono mountain.
  • Delaware Water Gap – Explore the 67,000-acre national Recreation Area surrounding the spectacular Delaware Water Gap.
  • Delaware River – Canoe, kayak, or paddle down the unspoiled and scenic Delaware river.
    • We recommend Edge of the Woods Outfitters for canoe, kayak or raft river trips, as well as bike rentals
  • Olde Engine Works – Located in a machine shop dating back to 1903, this popular antique market offers 125+ vendors with a wide variety of products.
  • Camelback Mountain – In the summer, guests can enjoy Camelbeach Waterpark, zip lining, mountain coasters, and treetop adventures. In the fall, the resort features scenic chairlift rides, hiking, and stunning views of the autumn foliage.
