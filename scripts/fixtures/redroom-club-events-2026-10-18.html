Dark Rooms:Jackie Cox

This October, things are getting very dark… and very dirty!
Back at the Red Room for her third Spooky Bear (and somehow still allowed back), Jackie Cox invites you to DARKROOMS her filthiest adults-only cabaret yet! Loosely inspired (and we cannot emphasize loosely enough) by the hit horror film Backrooms, Jackie slips through the wrong door (which, historically, is how most of her best nights begin) and plunges headfirst into a shadowy labyrinth of sex, camp, strange men, and stains best left unidentified.
But don’t worry, scaredy-cats: DARKROOMS is only as scary as your clinic checkup will be the week after Halloween... Expect killer vocals, filthy comedy, ridiculous costumes, spooky silliness, and things that go bump in the night. There’s just one rule: absolutely no safe word. (We tried “Wellfleet” but that only made things worse)
It’s part horror movie, part filthy cabaret, and part Dick Dock with a plot! Where the biggest fright of the night might just be when the lights come on.
Come for the camp. Stay for what happens in the dark!
