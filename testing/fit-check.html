Zoom
Tap the first point on the plan.

Drag a piece to move it; double-tap it for a quarter turn; drag the round handle on a selected piece to turn it any amount (it catches every 15°); ↺ and ↻ turn it 5° (hold to keep going, or type an exact angle under Add a piece); the arrows nudge it an inch. Drag the plan itself to pan, pinch to zoom. Edges snap together when close; red means two pieces overlap. Undo takes back the last change. Arrow keys nudge 1", Shift+arrow 1', R rotates, [ and ] turn 5°, Delete removes, Ctrl+scroll zooms. The tool knows the scale, not the walls, so check those by eye.
